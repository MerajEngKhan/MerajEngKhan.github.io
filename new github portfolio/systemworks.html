01 / overview

Design, controls, and commissioning.

My SystemWorks experience brought together mechanical design reviews, controls reviews, functional test development, and commissioning support.

As a Mechanical Engineering Co-op and Intern in West Des Moines, I worked with drawings, specifications, BAS sequences, and functional tests for HVAC and piping systems. I also supported equipment verification, recorded technical findings, and tracked issues with the commissioning team.

I completed a co-op from May 2025 through January 2026 and received a return offer for a summer internship from May through August 2026.

Engineering scope

Review how a system is designed, understand how it should be controlled, develop tests of its intended behavior, and document the findings.

02 / reviews

Understand the requirements and the sequence.

Mechanical design reviews

Reviewed engineering drawings, specifications, and mechanical system information. Connected the design documentation with equipment requirements and intended operation.

Controls reviews

Reviewed building automation system (BAS) sequences of operation and equipment integration. Considered how controls influence the behavior of the mechanical system.

These reviews provided the context for testing: what equipment is expected to do, how it should be controlled, and what performance needs to be evaluated.

03 / test development

Build the tests that make performance reviewable.

Developing functional tests was part of my work, alongside supporting their use in commissioning.

I developed functional tests for mechanical systems and supported functional performance testing. This connected the design and controls reviews to the evaluation of equipment operation.

  1. Review the intended operation

    Use design information and control sequences to understand the system behavior being evaluated.

  2. Develop the functional tests

    Build functional tests around the operation that needs to be checked during commissioning.

  3. Support performance evaluation

    Participate in functional testing and review equipment integration and mechanical system behavior.

  4. Record findings and follow-up

    Document test findings and deficiencies and track corrective actions with the project team.

04 / documentation

Make technical findings actionable.

I maintained deficiency logs, tracked corrective actions, and prepared technical reports. I coordinated findings with engineers, clients, and contractors to support follow-up and issue resolution.

Clear technical records

Record observations and test findings so the team can understand the issue and follow its resolution.

Cross-team communication

Connect design information, control sequences, and observed operation when discussing the next action with project stakeholders.

What this experience adds to my engineering work

I consider design intent, control logic, testability, and documentation together when approaching a mechanical system.
