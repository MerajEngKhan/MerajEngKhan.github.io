01 / challenge

Make the parts work as one system.

Design and assemble a 1:10-scale RC vehicle that connects custom parts with real motors, electronics, bearings, and wheels.

The challenge is the integration. A part can look correct in CAD while its clearance, mounting, or alignment still needs adjustment during assembly. This personal project connects digital modeling with physical fit and functional checks.

My role

I developed SolidWorks parts and assemblies, produced custom 3D-printed components, and integrated the mechanical and electrical hardware into an iterative prototype.

02 / design

Start with the interfaces.

Chassis architecture, steering, and drivetrain packaging guided the layout. Assembly mates, dimensional constraints, and interference checks helped evaluate how the components relate before fabrication.

InterfaceDesign focus
Motor & gearsPackage the drive components and check alignment at the gear interface.
Wheels, axles & bearingsCoordinate mating dimensions and clearances for assembly and rotation.
Steering & chassisArrange component interfaces while allowing space for steering motion.
Battery, servo & hardwareIntegrate the supporting components and keep connections accessible.
Printed componentsRefine geometry for practical fabrication, assembly, and fit.
03 / iteration

Model. Print. Assemble. Refine.

  1. Model and package

    Use SolidWorks parts and assemblies to develop the chassis and component interfaces. Check clearances and relationships between the motor, servo, battery, and wheel assemblies.

  2. Fabricate and integrate

    3D-print custom components and assemble the steering, drivetrain, and wheel interfaces with the selected hardware.

  3. Inspect and revise

    Check fit, alignment, and functionality during assembly. Use observations from the physical prototype to refine part geometry and connections.

Engineering takeaway

Physical assembly is part of the design process. Fit checks turn an on-screen assembly into evidence about clearances, alignment, and serviceability.

04 / outcome

An integrated physical prototype.

The build brings together a chassis, steering layout, drive components, electronics, and 3D-printed parts. It demonstrates a workflow from CAD through fabrication and mechanical integration.

The project remains an iterative personal build. Current work centers on fit, alignment, and functional refinement.
