01 / context

Contribute to a lightweight vehicle.

Support structural development for the PRISUM Solar Car team at Iowa State University.

A solar vehicle brings weight, strength, and packaging into the same design problem. My contributions focused on material selection and door-support components within the team’s broader vehicle structure.

Scope of my work

I contributed to material selection, lightweight structural design, and 3D-printed door-support brackets. The vehicle structure shown here is a team project.

02 / materials

Balance strength, weight, and integration.

I evaluated and selected 6061-T6 aluminum for lightweight structural applications based on its material properties. The work connected material choice with the team’s need to balance component strength and weight.

Material properties

Consider strength and weight together when assessing a structural material.

System context

Consider how a component fits into the surrounding vehicle structure and its mechanical interfaces.

03 / brackets

Design around a practical interface.

I designed custom 3D-printed door-support brackets with the aim of reducing frame loading and component weight. The contribution combined additive manufacturing with mechanical integration and lightweight design considerations.

Working on a smaller component within a larger vehicle reinforced the importance of understanding how local design choices affect the surrounding assembly.

Engineering takeaway

A useful team contribution can be a focused component or material decision. The goal is to make that contribution fit the larger system.
